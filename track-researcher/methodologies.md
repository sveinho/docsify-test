This module covers the core frameworks for data gathering, ethical guidelines, and how we structure our research sprints before handing project data over to management.

---

## 🛠️ Required Tools for Researchers

Before you begin your analysis, ensure you have access to the following platforms:
*   **NVivo / ATLAS.ti** (For qualitative data coding)
*   **SPSS / RStudio** (For quantitative statistical analysis)
*   **Institutional Survey Account** (For GDPR-compliant data collection)

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Research Setup

Follow these three steps to initiate a new research project:

### Step 1: Define the Scope
Establish your hypothesis and select the mixed-methods approach best suited for the target demographic.

### Step 2: Ethical Clearance
Submit your data processing agreement to the compliance officer before any interviews or surveys are distributed.

### Step 3: Data Ingestion
Configure your secure storage environment to automatically pseudonymize incoming participant responses.

## 🖥️ Operational Workflows per Role

Depending on your role, your interaction with this research phase will differ. Select your tab below to view customized instructions:

<!-- tabs:start -->

#### **Researcher**
You are responsible for gathering data and maintaining the integrity of the data source.
* Ensure all interview transcriptions are stored in encrypted volumes.
* Apply standardized thematic coding tags to all qualitative feedback.

#### **Manager**
As a manager, you track the timeline and resource allocation of these active research sprints.
* Use the project board to monitor milestone completions.
* Budget for transcription services and software licensing requirements.

#### **Trainer**
As a trainer, you utilize the finalized insights and case studies generated by the researchers.
* Extract anonymized quotes to create realistic scenarios for workshop participants.
* Do not use raw, unvetted datasets during live presentation sessions.
