### 💡 Navigation Tips for Researchers

* **Badges:** As you browse the materials, look for the blue <span class="badge track-researcher">Researcher</span> label at the top of the articles. If you see green or red labels, it means that specific element is tailored for other roles, but you are still welcome to read it.
* **Tabs:** When encountering pages with tabbed content, simply click on the **Researcher** tab to get the methodology and data-focused instructions.
* **Global Search:** Use the search bar at the top of the left menu to find specific research terms, tools, or templates across all tracks instantly.
