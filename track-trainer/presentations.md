## 🗣️ Delivery Methods by Target Audience

Select your focus area to view instructions on how to handle different learning dynamics:

<!-- tabs:start -->

#### **Academic / Research Focus**
When training groups who require deep methodological understanding:
* Lean heavily on the datasets provided in the [Research Methodologies](/track-researcher/methodologies.md) module.
* Allocate 30% of the session time to Q&A regarding data validity and sourcing.

#### **Operational / Management Focus**
When working with project coordinators and business leaders:
* Focus on high-level takeaways, implementation speed, and ROI.
* Use active case studies that highlight workflow optimizations.

#### **Train-the-Trainer**
When preparing other educators to deliver this specific curriculum:
* Focus on facilitation techniques, common participant pitfalls, and how to grade final evaluation metrics.
