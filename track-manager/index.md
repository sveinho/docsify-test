### ⚡ How to Navigate the Manager Track

* **Badges:** Keep an eye out for the green <span class="badge track-manager">Manager</span> label at the top of the pages. If an article also displays a blue badge, it indicates shared foundation material that is highly relevant to your workflow tracking.
* **Tabs:** Several of the operational modules utilize tabs. Make sure you select the **Manager** tab to reveal the correct framework setups, resource management tips, and KPIs.
* **Cross-References:** The entire portal is linked together. If a management step requires deep methodological context, you can click the link to jump directly to the Researcher track, and your sidebar will update automatically.
