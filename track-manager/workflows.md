Welcome to the Manager track! This module guides you through establishing operational workflows, setting up progress tracking, and aligning timelines between research outputs and training schedules.

---

## 📈 Management Pre-requisites

Ensure you have access and administrator rights to the following tracking suites:
*   **Jira / Linear** (For agile sprint planning)
*   **Miro** (For cross-functional roadmapping)
*   **Resource Allocation Sheets** (For budget and hour tracking)

## 🔄 Tracking Sprints vs. Training Cohorts

Choose the tab that represents your current operational focus:

<!-- tabs:start -->

#### **Research Phase Tracking**
When managing the researchers, focus on blocking issues and external dependencies:
* Host weekly syncs to monitor data gathering progress.
* Ensure compliance reviews do not stall active data collection phases.

#### **Training Phase Logistics**
When transitioning to the training delivery phase, shift focus to user onboarding and venue coordination:
* Allocate trainers based on cohort size.
* Lock the curriculum content freeze two weeks prior to the live launch.

#### **Trainer Context**
*For Trainers:* See details regarding delivery formats and evaluation rubrics in the [Presentation Frameworks](/track-trainer/presentations.md) guide.
