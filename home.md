### 💡 How to Navigate the Portal

*   **Badges:** At the top of most articles, you will see colored labels like <span style="background:#28a745; color:white; padding:2px 6px; border-radius:3px; font-size:0.8em;">Manager</span>. These indicate which track the information element belongs to.
*   **Tabs:** Some pages contain tabbed sections. Click on your specific role tab to reveal customized instructions and examples.
*   **Search:** Use the search bar at the top of the sidebar to find specific topics across all three learning paths instantly.
